Animated walkthrough

The intention

Make the journey from opening the website to approving an online giving transaction easier to follow.

The craft

The phone gives the process a familiar frame. Enlarged interface details and on-screen steps keep the action readable, while each transition carries the viewer toward the next decision.

Read the visual description

The film demonstrates opening the FECI website, scrolling to Give now, choosing a giving type, entering an amount, reviewing payment methods, choosing Mobile Money, entering and checking the payment number, and approving the transaction on a phone. The animation uses interface movement, phone framing and on-screen instructions to explain the sequence.
